ULBs Market Readiness Assessment

The assessment, based on ULBs’ financial health, assesses their readiness to avail municipal borrowings for infrastructure development.

  • 4,800+ Urban Local Bodies evaluated on a composite score of 100
  • Based on three years of Financial data
  • Evaluated on four guiding pillars: Revenue Generation, Expenditure Composition and Liquidity and Debt Management
