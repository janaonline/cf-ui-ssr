About the Assessment

What does market readiness mean?

^

Market readiness shows how prepared a city is to access market-based borrowing based on its financial performance. It is not a credit rating, but a precursor that can help cities and external stakeholders assess if a city is positioned to pursue a formal credit rating or participation in the Urban Challenge Fund.

What is the basis for this assessment and scoring logic?

^

The scoring framework has been developed in consultation with the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs and evaluates key aspects of financial performance using standardized indicators.

The assessment for each city is based on audited financial statements and property tax data submitted on the City Finance platform.

What do the bands and scores mean?

^

Cities are grouped into six bands from A1 to D based on their score out of 100. Each band represents a defined level of preparedness. The "distance to next milestone" column indicates how close a city is to the next band and helps guide next steps.
